Privacy & terms

Last updated 29 October 2026. In plain words, here is what we do with your details and what to expect from our classes.

Who we are

Sasi Wonder Cakes is a home bakery in Pondicherry, India. We run live online baking classes on Google Meet.

What we collect

  • Your name, WhatsApp number and email, and a note if you add one (for example an allergy).
  • Which class you booked, your booking reference, and when you accepted these terms.
  • Whether and when you paid. We never see or store your card, bank or UPI PIN details. You pay in your own UPI app, and we only see in our own UPI or bank app that a payment arrived.

Why we use it

To hold your seat, confirm your payment, email you the Google Meet link and class-day reminder, and message you on WhatsApp about your class. We do not use your details for advertising, and we never sell them.

Who can see it

  • Our small team who run the classes.
  • The services we use to do this: Cloudflare to run this website and store your booking, Google (Gmail and Meet) to send emails and run the class, and WhatsApp to message you.
  • When you open this website, Google Fonts loads our fonts, so Google can see that your device visited.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it to run your class and keep our records. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.
